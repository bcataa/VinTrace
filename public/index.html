What is the structure of a Vehicle Identification Number?

1 Country
2-3 Manufacturer
4-8 Vehicle Attributes
9 Security Code
10 Model Year
11 Assembly Plant
12-17 Serial Number

Note: Pre-1981 vehicles may have VINs of different lengths.

🔍

What is a VIN?

A Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) is a unique 17-character code that serves as a vehicle's fingerprint. It contains information about the vehicle's manufacturer, model, year, and specifications.

📋

The Anatomy of a VIN

Each position in a VIN reveals specific information. From the country of origin to the serial number, every character has meaning and follows international standards.

Why VIN Decoding Is Important
